Engine Part

The Porsche 991 GT3 RS is powered by a 4.0-liter naturally aspirated flat-six engine (codenamed MDG.G).

Key Specs:
• Displacement: 4.0 L (3,996 cc)
• Power: 520 hp (383 kW) at 8,250 rpm
• Torque: 346 lb-ft (470 Nm) at 6,000 rpm
• Redline: 8,800 rpm (9,000 rpm with overrun)
• Engine layout: Rear-mounted, flat-six (Boxer)
• Valvetrain: DOHC with VarioCam Plus
• Induction: Naturally aspirated with individual throttle bodies
• Crankshaft: Forged steel, lightweight
• Pistons: Forged aluminum
• Connecting rods: Titanium
• Lubrication: Dry sump (8.5 L capacity)
• Weight: Approximately 160 kg (353 lbs)

Why it's special:
This engine is considered the peak of Porsche's naturally aspirated flat-six development for road cars. It shares DNA with the 918 Spyder's engine and the RSR race car.
